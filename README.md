# Netflix Titles Analysis

**Dataset:** Netflix Movies and TV Shows (Kaggle), a sample of 2,119 titles.

**Tool:** Excel (COUNTIF formulas and a column chart).

## What I did
Counted titles by type (Movie or TV Show) and by country, then charted the top five countries.

## Findings
1. Movies (1,381) outnumber TV Shows (738) by almost 2 to 1.
2. The United States leads with 863 titles, far ahead of India (172) and the United Kingdom (168).
3. Canada has 96 titles and Japan has 77.

**Note:** A title made in several countries is counted once for each country.

## Recommendation
Netflix's catalog leans toward movies and US content. Adding more international TV shows could widen its variety.
